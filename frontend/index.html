Four steps.Zero manuals.

  1. 01

    Pick a chore

    Leaky tap, dead bulb, sticky table. Tap the spot in your home or just type it.

  2. 02

    Show us how

    Sketch the motion, or put on a headset and do it once yourself. That's your demo.

  3. 03

    Agents design the robot

    They pick the base, arms and hands that fit your space, and tell you why each part won.

  4. 04

    Watch it in sim

    It does the job in a physics simulation first. If it fumbles, the agents fix it before anything gets built.
