Glove fingers, live

connecting...

Raw fingertip readings, 0 to 4095. Not newtons: each sensor has its own curve until it is calibrated. Above about 3500 a sensor barely moves. A line that never leaves 0 while you press is a sensor or wire that is not connected. A gap in the lines is the glove itself going quiet: "restarted" counts the times its ESP32 rebooted, which takes 2 to 3 seconds each.
