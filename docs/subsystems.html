13 cards cover all three jobs through both kinds of doorway, plus structure.counterweight if the stability check fails.

Checks the new parts need

  • Tip-over: centre of mass inside the wheelbase with the reach at full height and full payload.
  • Joint torque: each motor's rating against its load at the worst pose.
  • Stowed envelope: width, length, height against the narrowest passage.
  • Runtime: battery against route length and task time.
  • Force band: grip and wrist force covers the band measured at home, with margin.
